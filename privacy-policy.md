Privacy Policy – Kingdom Drop
Last updated: 11 August 2026
Kingdom Drop is a mobile game published by SPS Games.
This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Kingdom Drop.
Information stored on your device
Kingdom Drop stores certain game information locally on your device, including game progress, settings, scores, unlocked content and in-game currency balances.
This information is used solely to provide the game's functionality and is not transmitted to SPS Games' own servers.
Kingdom Drop does not require users to create an account or provide their name, email address or other personal details in order to play.
Advertising
Kingdom Drop may display advertisements using Google AdMob, a service provided by Google.
Google and its advertising partners may collect and process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, device information and information relating to advertisement interactions for purposes including displaying, measuring and improving advertisements.
Depending on your location and applicable privacy laws, you may be given options relating to personalised and non-personalised advertising.
Further information about how Google processes information in connection with advertising is available through Google's own privacy policies and advertising documentation.
In-app purchases
Kingdom Drop may offer optional in-app purchases.
Purchases on Android are processed through Google Play. SPS Games does not directly collect or store your payment card or banking details.
Google may process information relating to purchases in accordance with its own privacy policies.
Analytics
Kingdom Drop does not currently use a separate analytics service operated by SPS Games.
Services provided by third parties, such as Google Play and Google AdMob, may generate diagnostic, advertising or service-related information in accordance with their own policies.
Children's privacy
Kingdom Drop is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children.
SPS Games does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through Kingdom Drop.
Data sharing
SPS Games does not sell users' personal information.
Information may be processed by third-party service providers necessary to provide advertising, purchases and distribution of the game, including Google.
Data security
Game progress and settings handled directly by Kingdom Drop are stored locally on the user's device.
Third-party services used by Kingdom Drop are responsible for the security of information they process under their respective privacy policies.
Changes to this Privacy Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated if Kingdom Drop's features, advertising services, purchasing functionality or legal requirements change.
Any updates will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
